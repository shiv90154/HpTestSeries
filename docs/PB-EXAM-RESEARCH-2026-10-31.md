## 2. Punjab District Court Clerk — SSSC, Advt 37C/SSSC/PB/2026

Source: the 14-page Detailed Employment Notice dated 03/10/2026, https://sssc.gov.in/storage/notices/Advt dated 03.10.2026 Pb Clerk.pdf. **OFFICIAL, read in full text.**

- Posts: 1,270. A table splits them by category as on 31.08.2026 and as anticipated up to 28.02.2027. The table did not extract cleanly, so the 811 + 459 split seen on aggregators is **not confirmed**.
- Apply: 07/10/2026 4:00 pm to 04/11/2026 4:00 pm at www.sssc.gov.in. No later notice on the SSSC notice board (newest entry is the advertisement itself); an extension is not ruled out.
- Age as on 01.01.2026: 18–37; +5 years SC and BC/OBC of Punjab; +10 years PwBD of Punjab; serving government employees up to 45; ex-servicemen: military service plus 3 years.
- Qualification: BA or BSc (or equivalent); matriculation with Punjabi as a subject; proficiency in computer operation, on the closing date.
- Pay: ₹29,200 (Level 5).
- Fee (online, non-refundable): ₹710 for SC/BC/OBC/ESM/EWS of Punjab (₹550 facilitation + ₹160 exam fee); ₹875 for PwBD of Punjab (₹550 + ₹325); ₹1,200 for all others (₹550 + ₹650).
- Exam: online CBT, 2 hours, 100 marks. GK 50 objective; English Composition 20 objective; English subjective typed on screen 30 marks (essay 150 words 10, letter 5, précis 5, Punjabi to English translation 10). 1/4 mark negative for each wrong MCQ. Qualifying: 33% in each subject and 40% aggregate; the subjective part is checked only if GK is 33% or more. Marks normalised if there are several shifts.
- Then a qualifying Computer Proficiency Test: spreadsheet 10 marks, 10 minutes, 4 marks to pass; then English typing at 30 wpm (checked only if the spreadsheet is passed). Its marks do not count in merit. Merit is by CBT marks; ties go to the elder candidate.
- Exam date: not set; "can be conducted anywhere in Northern India on any suitable date(s)", published on sssc.gov.in only. A demo/mock test link goes up 15 days before the exam.
- Answer key objections cost ₹25 each.

Code status: the pattern, age, qualification, pay and CPT already in `prisma/exam-pages/punjab.ts` match this notice. Two FAQs (fee, exam date and demo test) were added from it today.

## 8. What this means for the site

1. Court Clerk is the only live drive where we hold the full official notice. It closes for applications on 4 Nov 2026, the CBT date is not out, and the notice promises a demo test 15 days before the exam.
2. PSSSB Advt 12/2026 (Clerk Legal, Clerk Accounts, Clerk IT, Driver, Translator) and 13/2026 (Law Officer, Legal Assistant, Accountant) closed in October with no exam date yet. They are the next likely PSSSB exams. Patterns are NOT READ, so do not build mocks until the PDFs are read by a person.
3. PPSC Senior Assistant is the largest open exam by candidates but its date is unconfirmed (November 2026 or January 2027). Confirm it on ppsc.gov.in before writing any page.
4. Assistant Lineman 2026 looks cancelled. Do not write pages for it.
5. Search Console shows people searching for PSSSB Clerk, PPSC Naib Tehsildar, PSPCL LDC and PPSC CDPO. No official notice for Naib Tehsildar, CDPO or PSPCL LDC was found on the pages read (not searched exhaustively).
